# CitizenSense - Annotation Guidelines

## 1. Purpose
These guidelines define how feedback samples will be labeled for the CitizenSense NLP project.

## 2. Complaint Categories

| Category | Definition |
|---|---|
| Roads / Infrastructure | Roads, potholes, streetlights, bridges and public infrastructure |
| Electricity | Power outages, voltage issues and electricity services |
| Water Supply | Water shortages, leakage and water quality |
| Sanitation | Garbage collection, drainage and cleanliness |
| Health | Public hospitals, clinics and healthcare services |
| Education | Public schools, colleges and educational facilities |
| Traffic | Traffic signals, congestion, road safety and transport |
| General | Public feedback that does not fit another category |

## 3. Sentiment Labels

- **Positive:** The feedback expresses satisfaction or appreciation.
- **Negative:** The feedback expresses dissatisfaction or reports a problem.
- **Neutral:** The feedback is informational or does not clearly express positive or negative emotion.

## 4. Priority Labels

- **High:** Urgent public safety concerns or serious service disruptions.
- **Medium:** Significant service problems that need attention but are not immediately dangerous.
- **Low:** Minor issues, suggestions or non-urgent requests.
